# How a family workbook is built

**Status: `[DEFINED]`.** Angry is the worked example; this is the shape the other
six follow. Written after the fact, from what the angry build actually needed and
from every correction it took to get there — a record, not a plan.

Angry produced 23 pages of workbook, 12 of the child's exploration book, 1 for
parents, and a 31-card deck shared by all seven families.

---

## Four outputs, one source

    python3 scripts/figure-workbook-cover.py <family>    # both covers
    python3 scripts/build-workbook-pdf.py <family>       # the three documents
    python3 scripts/build-card-deck.py [locale]          # the deck, all families

| File | Reader | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `<familia>-caderno.pdf` | the clinician | framing, strategies, guidance, records |
| `<familia>-exploracao.pdf` | the child | her own book, with its own cover |
| `<familia>-pais.pdf` | the family | one page, a letter, taken home |
| `baralho/baralho-<locale>.pdf` | the room | 31 cards, 90×120mm |

**Never write two sources.** Separate documents drift and a colleague ends up
with versions that disagree (D-170, D-172, D-188).

### The two files to edit

- `docs/materials/<familia>-caderno.md` — the prose.
- `docs/materials/<familia>-fichas.html` — the sheets, as
  `<section class="sheet" data-for="…" data-age="…">`.

Sheets are tagged `data-for="child|practitioner|parents"`; **untagged means
child**. Everything else — covers, figures, cards, guides — the scripts find by
name.

## The workbook, in the order a session happens

1. **Enquadramento** — what the feeling is, what it is for, and **the governing
   distinction of that family**. For angry: *anger is not aggression*. Find this
   before writing anything else; it is what the rest hangs on. Likely candidates:
   sadness → sadness is not depression; fear → fear is not danger; shame → shame
   is not guilt; boredom → boredom is not laziness.
2. **O que a criança encontra** — the interactive path, with her literacy line
   quoted.
3. **As estratégias, e o que as sustenta** — each graded, each with what it does
   not do.
4. **O esquema** — one figure explaining the thing families get wrong. Anger's is
   the arousal curve; another family's may be a different shape entirely. Drawn
   in code, **never generated**, and with **no numbers on the axes** — a graph
   with a scale looks like data.
5. **Perguntas exploratórias** — grouped by the strategy that prompts them, plus
   **one group per fine word**, each with its own caution.
6. **Dinâmicas gerais** — with the child, with the family present, between
   sessions.
7. **Ficha clínica** — five blocks: sessão · o que foi usado · observação ·
   leitura · plano. **Observation stays apart from interpretation**; on one line
   the difference is lost. By code, never by name.
8. **As palavras derivadas** — what each carries and what it hides.
9. **Fichas — orientação de aplicação** — generated by
   `scripts/build-sheet-guides.py`. One page per sheet, **beginning and ending on
   its own page**: age · base · objective · how to run it · what to notice ·
   cautions, then exploration questions, then **four dynamics banded by age**,
   then the session record. **The worksheets themselves are not bound here** —
   they are in the child's book, and a licence gives both files.
10. **Limites** — what the evidence does not reach.
11. **O que este material não faz.**

Annexes: the deck, and the five colouring pages. **Never the worksheets.**

## The child's exploration book

Its own cover, then:

1. **Identity page** — a mirror to draw herself in. **First name and age, and
   nothing else**: no surname, no school, no address. A sheet travelling between
   a clinic and a home must not identify a child to whoever finds it.
2. **Psychoeducation** — three statements and the schema, **nothing to fill in**.
   The workbook's own foundations in her language.
3. **The worksheets.**

## Choosing the sheets

**Per family, not to a grid.** Externalising suits angry and scared; the *lonely*
card almost certainly asks for something else. Angry's nine, as a menu:

| Sheet | Age | Base |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Psicoeducação | 6–9 | to read, nothing to fill |
| O que acontece antes | 7–9 | behavioural, triggers |
| [A Emoção] vem visitar | 4–7 | narrative, externalising |
| As palavras finas | 7–9 | the set together |
| One per fine word | 7–9 | each asking something different |
| Da próxima vez | 6–9 | problem-solving |
| Compor as coisas | 6–9 | repair |
| O que dizer aos pais | practitioner | parent-mediated |

**Distribute the ages deliberately** — not everything at one end. Each sheet
states its range.

**One sheet per fine word, each asking something different.** Three templates
with the name swapped is padding. Angry's ask: which feeling was it really
(chateado) · what repeats (irritado) · what happened afterwards (furioso).

## Rules that are not negotiable

**Never *você* or *vocês*.** Address the reader as *vós*, or name them — *os
pais*, *quem cuida*, *a criança*.

**Boxes, never ruled lines.** A line only takes writing, which shuts out the
youngest children and every child with a written-language difficulty — precisely
the child a psychologist has in front of her. **Every box says what is wanted**:
*escreve ou desenha o que se repete*.

**Assinala, never aponta.** Pointing leaves nothing behind, and the point of
paper is that she takes it with her.

**Nothing printed inside a box.** A corner motif was tried and rejected: it
looked good and took that corner away from the child.

**Boxes grow.** `data-lines` is a share of the leftover space, not a height. The
page fills itself; the child gets room to draw rather than room to sign her name.

**Reuse the deck.** Seven family cards and twenty-four fine cards exist. Generate
art only for what the deck cannot supply — an empty frame, three boxes, speech
bubbles, a mirror. **Structure is drawn in code**; a generated sheet with fields
comes back with deformed text in them.

**The child's page speaks to her, not about her.** The schema exists in two
versions for exactly this reason.

**The workbook sets smaller than the sheets** — 9.2pt against 10.5pt, and the
guides at 8.4pt. A technical document read by a professional against pages read
by a child.

**The parents' page is a letter.** Systemic framing and care for the adult;
**strategies belong in the intervention, not on the sheet that leaves the room**.
It closes itself — nothing on it asks a question needing someone present to
receive the answer. It ends relationally: *if something here does not fit your
house, tell me next time.*

**The child's cover carries the plain logo**, never the endorsed professional
lockup, and no licensing strapline or clinical disclaimer.

**No sorting by intensity.** It is the first dynamic anyone thinks of and the one
thing this material has refused throughout. Sort by frequency, by how hard it is
to say aloud, by who is there, by recognition. **Those yield clinical
information; sorting by size yields a number.**

## Grading, every time

Three levels: **estabelecido · razoável · prática**. The base goes on the label
beside the level — *base: externalização, terapia narrativa, prática* — never in
the structure. The workbook is organised by what is done with the child.

**No references written from memory.** State the evidence, mark it for
verification, cite nothing until checked.

**Bases permitted** (D-171): emotion-focused · systemic and family ·
parent–child interaction **principles only**, never implying the protocol ·
structured play · narrative · sparing mindfulness. **Out:** psychodynamic
(becomes interpretation) · trauma-focused (presumes a frame a workbook cannot).

Say *intervenção sistémica*, not FBT — FBT names the Maudsley family treatment
for eating disorders specifically.

## Before calling a family done

**Look at every printed page, not the source.** Every fault in the angry build
was invisible in the file and obvious on the sheet:

- an explanatory HTML comment printed as body text
- a guidance page stranding one paragraph on an extra sheet
- a near-white background printing as a grey panel behind every image
- emotion cards stretched wide by a flex column
- boxes vanishing silently because a regex assumed no extra attributes
- empty heading rows printing as grey stripes, and writing cells with no border

Then check: each sheet states an age · every box says what is wanted · nothing on
the child's pages talks about her instead of to her · the parents' page closes
itself · each guide sits alone on its page · no *você* anywhere.
