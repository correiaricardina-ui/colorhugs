# Triste

## Two rules specific to the sad family

**Nobody in these drawings is sad.** Not a tear, not a downturned mouth, not a
hunched back. The pages are about *company*, not about sadness — and a drawing
of a weeping child would do on paper exactly what D-098 promised the product
would never do: report what she chose to whoever finds the sheet. A filename can
be neutral; a crying figure cannot.

Faces are calm, soft, content — the ordinary kawaii face of the rest of the
library.

**Two of the six are already drawn** and are reused rather than remade: the
bench (*contar a alguém*) and the quiet corner (*um bocadinho no meu sítio*).
Four are new.

> Note carried from `HANDOFF.md`: the quiet corner is one of the three pages
> that predate the house style being applied to colouring pages, and is on the
> list to be redrawn. Reusing it here does not close that.

## The style block, at the top of every prompt in this file

```
STYLE — KAWAII, JELLYCAT SOFT-TOY, COZY
Everything is drawn as if it were a soft plush toy: shapes are fat, full and
squashy, slightly lumpy and imperfect rather than neat and symmetrical, as
though sewn by hand and lightly overstuffed. Short, chubby limbs. Large rounded
heads. No sharp points, no straight edges, no thin spikes — even a leaf or a
feather is plump and soft.

Warm and cozy in feeling: calm, gentle and huggable, never bright, spiky or
loud.

THICK, BOLD black outline of even weight throughout, the confident chunky
outline of a sticker, with a slight hand-drawn wobble rather than a perfect
line. A thin, delicate or varying line is WRONG. A neat, technical or generic
colouring-book line is WRONG.

Nothing rigid, geometric, symmetrical or mechanically neat. No realistic
proportions. No fine or elegant lines.

No stitching, no seams, no sewn lines, no fabric tags or labels — a soft toy is
the feeling, not the subject.

COLOURING PAGE — BLACK LINE ON PURE WHITE
Pure white background. Black outlines only. No grey, no shading, no hatching,
no stippling, no texture fill, no gradient, no colour anywhere.

SEALED LINES — SAY IT THREE WAYS
Every internal dividing line must start on another line and end on another
line. A line that stops in the middle of a white area is WRONG, because paint
poured into one part will run into every other part. Think of it as a wall in a
room: a wall that stops halfway across is not a wall.

AT LEAST EIGHT separate enclosed areas that a child can fill with a different
colour, each one a good size — not eight little specks.

NO TEXT OF ANY KIND. No words, no letters, no numbers, no numerals, no signs,
no labels, no writing on anything in the picture.

FORBIDDEN, ONE BY ONE: no eyebrows. No nostrils. No ears on the faces. No
teeth. No tongues. No eyelashes. No freckles. No cheek blush lines. No shading
under the chin. No wrinkles. No fingernails. No patterns on the clothes.

THE GROUND LINE: a single soft line spanning the whole width of the picture,
edge to edge. It must not stop short of either edge. Think of it as the
horizon.
```

## 1 · Alguém ao pé de mim, sem falar

*The strongest-supported of the six, and the one adults do least. It has to be
unmistakably about presence rather than about talking, or it becomes the bench
page again.*

```
[STYLE BLOCK]

THE SCENE
Two figures sitting side by side on the floor, close together, both facing the
same way — out towards the viewer, not towards each other. This is the whole
point of the picture: they are together and NOT talking. Two figures facing
each other would be a conversation, and this drawing is not a conversation.

One is a child, smaller. One is a grown-up, larger. Their shoulders are
touching.

Every figure MUST have hair — a big rounded soft mass of it. Bald is wrong.
Give the child hair of one shape and the grown-up hair of a clearly different
shape, so a child colouring can tell them apart.

Every figure MUST have visible clothing with a separate top and separate
trousers, so each is its own enclosed area.

Both faces are calm and content. Nobody is sad. No tears. No downturned mouth.

BEHIND THEM: a large soft cushion each, and a plump rounded plant in a fat pot
to one side.

FORBIDDEN, ONE BY ONE: no speech bubble. No hearts floating in the air. No
stars. No sparkles. No book. No phone. No table.
```

## 2 · Uma coisa macia

*The most universal of the six, and the one that needs nobody to be available.*

```
[STYLE BLOCK]

THE SCENE
A thick folded blanket, drawn fat and overstuffed like a cushion, lying on the
floor. On top of it sits one plush toy bear.

The blanket MUST be folded so that it shows three separate stacked layers, each
one its own enclosed area, with the fold lines running from the left edge of
the blanket to the right edge of the blanket and touching the outline at both
ends. A fold line that stops in the middle of the blanket is wrong.

The bear MUST have a face: two round dot eyes and one small curved mouth, and
nothing else on the face. The blanket MUST NOT have a face — it is a blanket.

The bear MUST have a separate round muzzle patch, a separate round tummy patch,
two separate round ear shapes, two separate arms and two separate legs, each
sealed as its own area.

Beside the blanket, one fat rounded mug, and one small plump folded cloth.

FORBIDDEN, ONE BY ONE: no stripes on the blanket. No checks. No polka dots. No
tassels. No fringe. No stitching. No steam from the mug. No hearts. No stars.
No sparkles.
```

# Assustado

**One new page.** Four of anger's five cross intact — the bench, the hand, the
feather, the jumping child — because fear also rises and has to come down.
**The fifth, the quiet corner, is barred**: for fear, leaving the situation *is*
avoidance, and the family's distinction says avoidance is what keeps it (D-238).

What this page has to do is unusual: **it is the only page in the library that
carries a mechanism rather than an action.** The ladder itself cannot be on
screen — it opens, and a child alone has nobody to receive it — so this page is
how the screen says the thing the ladder does. **She looks at it. She is asked
for nothing.**

The same rule as the sad family applies and matters more here: **nobody in the
drawing is frightened.** No wide eyes, no cowering, no hands over the face. A
frightened figure on paper reports what she chose to whoever finds the sheet, and
it also teaches the wrong thing — the picture is about arriving, not about fear.

### Chegar devagar · `stepping-stones.png`

```
[STYLE BLOCK]

THE SCENE
A row of five fat round stepping stones crossing from the left of the picture to
the right, like a path. On the third stone stands one child, mid-step. At the
far end, sitting on the ground beyond the last stone and waiting, one small
plush cat.

The child MUST be looking towards the cat and MUST be smiling calmly. She is not
frightened. No wide eyes. No hands over the face. No cowering. No shaking lines.

Every figure MUST have hair — a big rounded soft mass of it. Bald is wrong. The
child MUST have visible clothing with a separate top and separate trousers, each
its own enclosed area.

The five stones MUST each be a separate enclosed area, fat and rounded like
cushions, and they MUST be clearly different sizes and heights from one another
so they do not read as a numbered row. NO NUMBERS ON THE STONES. No marks, no
dots, no counting of any kind.

The cat MUST have two separate rounded ears, one separate rounded muzzle, one
separate round tummy patch and one fat rounded tail, each sealed as its own area.

BEHIND: two plump rounded bushes, one on each side, each its own area.

THE GROUND LINE: a single soft line spanning the whole width of the picture,
edge to edge, running beneath the stones. It must not stop short of either edge.
Think of it as the horizon.

FORBIDDEN, ONE BY ONE: no numbers. No arrows. No footprints. No dotted trail. No
water under the stones. No hearts. No stars. No sparkles. No speech bubble. No
sun with a face.
```

**Why the stones must differ in size:** five identical stones in a row is a
scale, and a scale is what this family's approach sheet was allowed to avoid by
ordering situations rather than levels (D-237). Uneven stones read as a path.
Even ones read as rungs.

# Refazer — três páginas que precedem a regra do estilo

`ART-DIRECTION.md` records that the house style was assumed to govern stickers
only, and that it produced **three colouring pages of generic line art**. They
were measurable, and the measurement was the wrong one: ink share was read as
line weight. Looked at against the pages accepted since, the fault is not
thickness — `counting-fingers` is the thickest page in the library at 10.8px.
**The fault is that nothing in them is plush.**

- **`quiet-corner` is the worst**, and it fails on an explicit rule: it has a
  straight wall, a straight skirting and a straight window edge. *No straight
  edges* is not a preference in this project.
- **`counting-fingers`** is a bare flat hand. Nothing about it is overstuffed,
  and it has no warmth at all.
- **`floating-feather`** is the closest to passing — its clouds and its face are
  right — but the feather has a straight central shaft and ruled diagonal veins,
  which is the technical line the style excludes.

**Two of the three are in the scared family** (the hand, the feather); the
cushions are in angry and sad. **Generate all four pages of this section in one
conversation** — including `stepping-stones` — because the first five pages ever
made came from two sessions and arrived in two styles (D-140).

**The subject must not change.** These are replacements, not new pages: a hand
stays a hand, a feather a feather, cushions cushions. The data, the names and
the meanings all stay where they are.

### A minha mão · `counting-fingers.png`

```
[STYLE BLOCK]

THE SCENE
One open hand, palm towards the viewer, fingers spread wide. Drawn as a soft
plush mitten rather than as a real hand: fat, overstuffed, rounded at every
point, with short chubby fingers that are wider at the tip than at the base.

**NO FACE.** No eyes, no mouth, no cheeks. This is the child's own hand, and a
hand with eyes stops being hers.

Each of the five fingers MUST be closed off at its base by a line that runs from
one side of the finger to the other and touches the outline at both ends, so
that each finger is its own enclosed area a child can fill with a different
colour. A finger that is open at the base is WRONG, because paint poured into it
runs into the palm and the whole hand becomes one colour.

The palm MUST be its own enclosed area. At the wrist there MUST be a thick soft
cuff, like a rolled sock, and it MUST be its own enclosed area.

NO NUMBERS ANYWHERE. No numerals beside the fingers, none on them, none
anywhere in the picture. A previous version of this page passed a check on the
number of fillable areas only because the generator had drawn the numerals 1 to
5 beside the fingers and five hollow digits counted as five areas — while the
hand itself was still one region and still gave a child one colour.

FORBIDDEN, ONE BY ONE: no numbers. No fingernails. No knuckle lines. No palm
creases. No wristwatch. No bracelet. No sleeve. No second hand. No arrows.
```

### Pena · `floating-feather.png`

```
[STYLE BLOCK]

THE SCENE
One large feather drifting downwards, with four fat rounded clouds around it.

**The feather MUST be plump and soft**, like a stuffed cushion in the shape of a
feather. It MUST have a rounded, slightly curved, thick soft shaft — a straight
shaft is WRONG, and a thin one is WRONG. Think of a padded quill, not a drawn
one.

The feather MUST have three fat rounded barbs on each side, each one its own
enclosed area, each shaped like a plump leaf and each meeting the shaft at both
ends. Ruled diagonal veins are WRONG. Fine hair-like barbs are WRONG. Six soft
lobes is right.

The feather MUST have a face: two round dot eyes and one small curved mouth, and
nothing else on the face.

The four clouds MUST be fat, lumpy and rounded, each its own enclosed area, and
they MUST be clearly different sizes.

FORBIDDEN, ONE BY ONE: no eyebrows. No nose. No cheek blush lines. No sparkles.
No stars. No motion lines. No dotted trail. No bird. No sun.
```

### Almofadas · `quiet-corner.png`

```
[STYLE BLOCK]

THE SCENE
A soft corner on the floor: four fat cushions, one thick folded blanket, and one

**NO WALL, NO WINDOW, NO SKIRTING BOARD, NO CORNER OF A ROOM.** The previous

THE GROUND LINE: a single soft line spanning the whole width of the picture,
edge to edge, behind everything. It must not stop short of either edge. Think of

FORBIDDEN, ONE BY ONE: no wall. No window. No skirting board. No straight lines
of any kind. No stripes on the cushions. No checks. No polka dots. No tassels.
No fringe. No stitching. No faces. No hearts. No stars. No sparkles.
```

# Landing the files

## Where they go, and what to run
The generated images are dropped into `artwork/colouring/strategies/`, named
exactly:

| File | Becomes |
| --- | --- |
| `sitting-beside.png` | *Alguém ao pé de mim, sem falar* |
| `soft-blanket.png` | *Uma coisa macia* |
| `dog-beside.png` | *Um bicho* |
| `keepsake-shelf.png` | *Uma coisa que me lembra alguém* |
| `stepping-stones.png` | *Chegar devagar* (assustado) |
| `counting-fingers.png` | *A minha mão* — substitui a existente |
| `floating-feather.png` | *Pena* — substitui a existente |
| `quiet-corner.png` | *Almofadas* — substitui a existente |

The filenames describe the picture and never the feeling (D-120): the child
downloads the PDF and the file that lands in her house must not report what she
chose.

Then:

    python3 scripts/prepare-colouring.py

It writes a WebP and an A4 PDF for each, and prints the numbers per page:
fillable areas, stroke width, ink share.

## Before accepting a page

Run `scripts/prepare-colouring.py`. **It refuses what fails, rather than
reporting it and carrying on**, which is the only reason the rule is real.

Then check by measuring rather than by looking (D-149):

- **Stroke width at least 7px.** Ink share is the wrong measure — a simple
  drawing has little ink even with a fat stroke.
- **At least six sealed regions**, each at least 0.5% of the image. The prompt
  asks for eight because asking for the minimum reliably returns less.
- **Flood-fill each region and look for leaks.** This is the failure most likely
  to appear and the hardest to see by eye.
- Compare black ink against a page already accepted.
- **Then** look at it.
