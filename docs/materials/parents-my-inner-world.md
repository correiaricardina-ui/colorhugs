# Parents page — My Inner World

Interface copy for `/parents`, approved 2026-08-15 (D-098).

**Written in the first person and addressed to the parent** — "we show you
none of it", not "parents do not have access". The impersonal form reads like a
privacy policy, as though something were being hidden. Owning the decision out
loud is what turns it into an argument rather than a gap.

**It says why, and the why is about the child, not about us.** A parent who
reads *to protect privacy* thinks of legislation. A parent who reads *she will
say what she thinks she ought to feel* recognises his own daughter and
understands immediately.

**Nothing here needs validating against the privacy policy.** "That is all we
keep: the cards, in order" covers intensity, the body map, and anything added
later, and stays true however Q-010 is decided. Retention and location belong
in the privacy policy; this promise is not hostage to it.

## PT-PT

> ### O Meu Mundo Interior é da criança
>
> Nesta área a criança escolhe uma carta para dizer o que sente e, se quiser,
> aponta onde no corpo o sente. É tudo.
>
> **Não lhe mostramos nada do que ela escolheu.** Não há resumo semanal, não há
> gráfico de humor, não há aviso quando ela escolhe a mesma carta muitas vezes.
> Isto não é uma limitação que ainda não resolvemos — é uma decisão.
>
> Uma criança que sabe que está a ser observada escolhe o que acha que deve
> sentir, e não o que sente. No momento em que existe um relatório para si, o
> ColorHugs deixa de ter valor para ela.
>
> Ela pode olhar para trás e ver as últimas cartas que escolheu, e é assim que
> aprende que os sentimentos passam. Guardamos só isso: as cartas, por ordem. O
> resto acontece no momento e não fica.
>
> **O que lhe damos em vez disso:** o mesmo baralho de sete cartas para
> imprimir. Em cima da mesa, com a criança a escolher a sua carta e o adulto a
> escolher a dele. O que ela lhe quiser contar, conta-lhe a si — não a nós.
>
> Nada disto é avaliação. O ColorHugs não diagnostica, não avalia e não
> substitui acompanhamento psicológico.

## EN

> ### My Inner World belongs to the child
>
> Here a child picks a card to say how she feels and, if she wants, points to
> where in her body she feels it. That is all.
>
> **We show you none of it.** No weekly summary, no mood chart, no alert when
> she picks the same card many times over. This is not something we have yet to
> build — it is a decision.
>
> A child who knows she is being watched picks what she thinks she should feel
> rather than what she does. The moment there is a report for you, ColorHugs
> stops being worth anything to her.
>
> She can look back at the last few cards she chose, and that is how she learns
> that feelings pass. That is all we keep: the cards, in order. The rest happens
> in the moment and is not kept.
>
> **What we give you instead:** the same seven cards, to print. On the table,
> with the child choosing hers and the grown-up choosing his. Whatever she
> wants to tell you, she tells you — not us.
>
> None of this is assessment. ColorHugs does not diagnose, does not assess, and
> does not replace psychological care.

## Note on the printable

This promise is about the product, not about the child. If she fills in the
printed sheet and the parent sees it, she showed him. **The product does not
report; the child is not required to hide.**
