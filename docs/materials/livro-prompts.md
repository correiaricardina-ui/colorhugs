---

## Como usar este ficheiro

**Cada prompt são duas partes: o bloco comum, sempre igual, e a cena.** Copia o
bloco comum, cola a cena a seguir, e gera.

**Gera por lotes de quatro ou cinco na mesma conversa**, e **dá as duas imagens de
referência no princípio de cada conversa** — a cena 4 e a cena 15. É o que mais
aumenta a probabilidade de as personagens saírem iguais, mais do que qualquer
palavra do prompt.

**A regra que se aprendeu nas três tentativas da cena 4:** onde o tamanho ou a
posição importam, **descreve-se o enquadramento e não a medida.** *Três vezes mais
alto* falhou duas vezes; *a cabeça toca o topo e os outros ficam na metade de
baixo* pegou à primeira. Cada cena abaixo traz a sua instrução de composição por
esse motivo.

**O vazio precisa de composição, e não basta ausência.** Escrever *o vazio é o
assunto* produziu páginas com setenta por cento de chão liso, que se lêem como
página por acabar e não como solidão. **A correcção é aproximar o enquadramento
e baixar a câmara**: a personagem fica maior, o vazio continua lá, e a página
deixa de parecer inacabada. Onde uma cena for de solidão, é isso que se pede.

**Uma cena já aceite vai em cada lote, como âncora.** As cenas 8 e 9 foram
geradas em par e derivaram em par — saíram com uma sala castanha escura que
nenhuma outra tem. Um lote sem referência do que já existe deriva inteiro.

**Economia de elenco.** Só entram na imagem as personagens que a cena precisa. Uma
cena com sete personagens é sete oportunidades de erro; uma com três é três.

## O bloco comum — colar no início de todos

```
STYLE — KAWAII, JELLYCAT SOFT-TOY, COZY, IN COLOUR
Everything is drawn as if it were a soft plush toy: fat, full and squashy
shapes, slightly lumpy and imperfect rather than neat, as though sewn by hand
and lightly overstuffed. No sharp points, no straight edges, no thin spikes.

Warm and cozy: calm, gentle, huggable. Never bright, spiky or loud.

THICK, BOLD black outline of even weight on every object, with a slight
hand-drawn wobble. A thin, delicate or varying line is WRONG.

FLAT COLOUR, no gradients, no airbrush, no glossy highlights, no drop shadows
under the characters, no paper texture and no grain anywhere. The wall is one
flat colour and the floor is one flat colour, and nothing else.

CHILDREN'S PICTURE BOOK ILLUSTRATION, not a poster and not an app screen.

NO TEXT OF ANY KIND anywhere in the image. No words, no letters, no numbers, no
speech bubbles, no signs.

THE CAST — AND THEIR FACES NEVER CHANGE
Each character is a fat rounded heart-shaped body with a dimple at the top, two
short fat legs and two short fat arms, no neck and no separate head. The face is
on the body. Each has two oval rosy cheeks.

THERE IS NO WHITE OUTLINE AND NO STICKER RIM around the figures. A white rim
makes them look like cardboard cut-outs pasted onto a photograph. They are drawn
inside the scene, with only the black outline around them.

RED — angry. Downward-slanting eyebrows, mouth curved down. A small puff of
steam beside the head.
LIGHT BLUE — sad. Droopy downturned eyes, mouth curved down, ONE BLUE TEARDROP
on the cheek.
LILAC — frightened. Very large round eyes with a lot of white, open oval mouth.
PINK — ashamed. Eyes looking down and away, small tight mouth, ONE HAND COVERING
PART OF THE FACE.
OLIVE — bored. Half-closed eyes looking sideways, mouth a straight line, ONE
HAND RESTING AGAINST THE CHEEK.
MINT GREEN — calm. Eyes closed in gentle curves, small serene smile.
GOLDEN YELLOW — happy. Eyes closed in gentle curves, wide open smiling mouth.

THE FACES ARE FIXED AND MUST NOT CHANGE, whatever is happening in the scene.
Only the arms may move, and only where the scene requires it. The colours must
not change. The body shape must not change.

FORBIDDEN IN EVERY SCENE, ONE BY ONE: no white rim. No drop shadows. No texture
on walls or floors. No speech bubbles. No exclamation marks. No tears on anyone
but the blue one. No noses. No ears. No hair. No clothes. No separate fingers.
No eyebrows on anyone except the red one. No teeth. No tongues. No sparkles. No
stars. No hearts floating in the air.
```

## Cena 1 · Chega alguém

*Abre o livro. **A cena 16 repete esta exactamente**, com uma personagem a mais —
por isso vale a pena guardar esta imagem e usá-la como referência quando gerares
a última.*

```
THE SCENE
A small single-storey house seen from outside, in cross-section like a doll's
house: the front wall is missing so we see one large room inside. Plain flat
walls, a plain flat floor, and a few plump rounded pieces of furniture.

INSIDE, SIX CHARACTERS, each in a different part of the room, each occupied with
something of its own and none of them looking at the others: the RED, the LIGHT
BLUE, the LILAC, the PINK, the MINT GREEN and the GOLDEN YELLOW.

OUTSIDE, on the ground in front of the house, THE OLIVE ONE, standing still and
looking in. He is the only one outside.

COMPOSITION: the house fills most of the picture and the olive one is small and
alone at the bottom edge, clearly separated from it by empty ground. The
separation between inside and outside is the subject of the picture.

FORBIDDEN: no door and no doorway drawn — the front wall is simply absent. No
roof tiles. No windows with frames. No garden, no fence, no path.
```

## Cena 2 · O que é que vocês fazem?

```
THE SCENE
Inside the room. THE OLIVE ONE stands in the middle of the floor, looking
around.

The other six are spread around the edges of the room, each doing something
small and separate, and NOT ONE OF THEM IS LOOKING AT HIM.

COMPOSITION: the olive one is alone in the empty centre and the others are all
near the walls. The empty floor between him and them is the subject of the
picture.

FORBIDDEN: nobody looking at the olive one. No character touching another.
```

## Cena 5 · Depois de passar

*Segue-se directamente à cena 4, que já está feita. **O vermelho voltou ao tamanho
normal** — é a mesma personagem da cena anterior e a diferença de tamanho é o que
a cena diz.*

```
THE SCENE
The same room as before, later. The broken pieces of the fat rounded vase are
still on the floor, untouched.

THE RED ONE is back to normal size — the same height as the others — and sits on
the floor. His face is unchanged: downward-slanting eyebrows, mouth curved down,
and the small puff of steam beside his head is now much smaller.

THE MINT GREEN ONE sits on the floor beside him, close, both facing the same way,
neither looking at the other.

THE OLIVE ONE stands far away at the other side of the room, watching.

Only these three characters are in this picture.

COMPOSITION: the two seated characters are together on one side and the olive one
is small and distant on the other. The distance between them is the subject.

FORBIDDEN: the red one must NOT be large in this picture. No hands touching. No
character looking at another.
```

## Cena 6 · O Triste perdeu uma coisa

```
THE SCENE
Inside the room. THE LIGHT BLUE ONE sits hunched on the floor, arms hanging,
looking down at an open fat rounded box that is EMPTY. His face is exactly as
described, with the one blue teardrop.

THE OLIVE ONE stands right in front of him, up on his toes, both arms in the
air, in the middle of jumping. His face is unchanged — half-closed eyes looking
sideways, straight mouth — because his face never changes.

Only these two characters are in this picture.

COMPOSITION: the two are close together and facing each other. The olive one is
all movement; the blue one has not moved at all. The contrast between the two
postures is the subject.

FORBIDDEN: the olive one must NOT be smiling — his face is unchanged. No motion
lines. Nothing inside the box.
```

## Cena 8 · A porta

```
THE SCENE
A wide doorway in a plain flat wall, standing open, with a simple flat green
outdoor space visible beyond it.

THE LILAC ONE stands several steps back from the doorway, inside, small, with
both hands up at his face as always.

THE OLIVE ONE stands beside him, closer to the door, one arm out towards it.

Far away, through the doorway and outside, three other characters are small in
the distance: the RED, the PINK and the GOLDEN YELLOW.

COMPOSITION: the doorway is bright and the inside is not. The lilac one is far
from it, and the empty floor between him and the doorway is the subject of the
picture.

FORBIDDEN: no door leaf, no handle, no steps. No trees, no flowers, no clouds —
the outside is a plain flat green field and a plain flat sky.

## Cena 12 · Uma coisa boa

```
THE SCENE
Inside the room. THE GOLDEN YELLOW ONE stands in the middle and is ENORMOUS,
with both arms raised high.

COMPOSE THE PICTURE AROUND HIS SIZE. He must not fit comfortably in the frame:
the top of his raised arms touches or is cut off by the top edge of the image,
and every other character stands entirely in the lower part of the picture. If
all the figures fit comfortably side by side, the composition is WRONG.

The other six are gathered around his feet, small, all of them turned towards
him and looking up: the RED, the LIGHT BLUE, the LILAC, the PINK, the OLIVE and
the MINT GREEN.

FORBIDDEN: no sparkles, no stars, no confetti, no light rays around the yellow
one. No sun. No party decorations of any kind.

## Cena 13 · Depois de acabar

*A cena mais delicada do livro. **O Feliz continua com a cara dele** — o
sentimento não mudou, o que acabou foi a coisa. Uma cara triste aqui destrói o
sentido da cena.*

```
THE SCENE
Inside the same room, later in the day, in a warmer and dimmer flat colour.

THE GOLDEN YELLOW ONE sits alone in a corner on the floor, SMALLER THAN NORMAL —
about two thirds of his usual size — with his arms hanging down at his sides and
his shoulders low.

HIS FACE IS COMPLETELY UNCHANGED: eyes closed in gentle curves, wide open smiling
mouth, rosy cheeks. He is NOT sad, he is NOT crying, and his mouth is NOT turned
down. The smiling face on a slumped, small body is the entire point of this
picture, and changing his face would destroy it.

THE OLIVE ONE stands a little way off, looking at him.

Only these two characters are in this picture.

COMPOSITION: the yellow one is small in one corner and a large part of the
picture is empty floor and empty wall. The emptiness around him is the subject.

FORBIDDEN: no change to the yellow one's face. No tears. No downturned mouth. No
open eyes. No other characters.
```

## Ordem de geração recomendada

**Lote 1 — as duas do enquadramento repetido.** Cena 1 e cena 16, juntas e pela
ordem, para que a segunda possa usar a primeira como referência. **Se estas duas
não ficarem iguais, o fim do livro não funciona**, e vale a pena sabê-lo cedo.

**Lote 2 — as de duas personagens**, que são as mais fáceis e servem para
estabilizar: cenas 3, 6, 9, 10, 13.

**Lote 3 — as de três**: cenas 5, 7, 14.

**Lote 4 — as de sala cheia**: cenas 2, 8, 11, 12.

---

## O que verificar em cada lote

1. **As cores estão certas e não falta nenhuma personagem pedida.**
2. **A lágrima do azul, a mão na cara do rosa, a mão na bochecha do caqui** — as
   três que o gerador mais facilmente deixa cair.
3. **Nenhum rebordo branco.**
4. **Nenhum texto.**
5. **Onde há mudança de tamanho** — cenas 5, 10, 12, 13 — **é evidente?** Se for
   preciso comparar duas imagens para notar, não é evidente.

**E a verificação que atravessa tudo:** posta qualquer cena nova ao lado da cena 4
e da cena 15, **são as mesmas personagens?**
