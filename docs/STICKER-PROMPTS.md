# ColorHugs — Collectible Sticker Prompts

The rewards that fill My Sticker Book. Twenty-four for launch.

Status: `[PROPOSAL]`. The system below needs approving before the artwork is
worth generating.

---

## What these are, and what they are not

**Avatars** are the child on the screen: one each, line art, coloured by what
the child does. **Stickers** are the collection: many, arriving already
coloured, kept for ever.

Because they are rewards, they carry the **white die-cut rim** that the section
artwork has and the avatars deliberately do not. The rim says *cut out and
collectable*, which is exactly what these are.

---

## Three rules that shape the art

### 1. No text on any sticker

The moment a sticker reads "Explorer", it needs seven translations and seven
regenerations, for ever. **The name is rendered by the app underneath the
sticker**, from the language file. The artwork stays language-free and is drawn
once.

This is the single most expensive mistake available here, and it is invisible
until the second language.

### 2. Earned by participation and variety, never by performance

Nothing below is awarded for being right, fast, or better than anyone. A child
with learning difficulties collects exactly as many as the quickest child in
her class, because what is counted is having been there and having tried
things.

**And nothing counted here can be lost.** The return stickers count days a
child came back — cumulatively, with no penalty for gaps. A streak that breaks
is a punishment, and we do not do those.

### 3. Legible at 120px

They will sit in a grid in the sticker book, small. One clear idea per sticker,
big shapes, no fine detail — a sticker that needs to be enlarged to be
understood has failed.

## Shared prompt block

Paste this before every variant line.

```
A cute sticker illustration for a children's app.

STYLE
Bold black outline of even weight. Flat bright colours, no gradients, no
shading, no texture. Rounded, chunky, friendly shapes. Kawaii sticker style,
the same look as a glossy die-cut sticker.

DIE-CUT STICKER
The illustration sits on a thick white border that follows its outline, like a
cut-out sticker. Plain white background outside that border.

COMPOSITION
Square image. One single object or motif, centred, filling most of the frame.
Simple and bold, readable when shrunk to a small size. Even margin on all sides.

COLOURS
Use bright, saturated colours from this palette: coral red, sunny yellow, mint
green, sky blue, lilac purple, warm orange.

DO NOT INCLUDE
No text, no letters, no numbers, no words of any kind. No background scenery.
No people. No drop shadow.
```

## Check before accepting one

- [ ] **No text anywhere.** The most common failure — generators add letters to
      rosettes and books almost by reflex.
- [ ] White die-cut border present and even.
- [ ] Flat colour, no gradient or shading.
- [ ] One motif, centred, square, even margins.
- [ ] Still readable shrunk to 120px. Check it small before accepting it large.
- [ ] Line weight matches the others.

Generate all twenty-four in one session where possible; line weight and border
thickness drift between sessions more than anything else.

---

## What happens after

Background cut to transparency and canvas normalised, as with every other
asset. Names and descriptions live in the language file, not in the artwork.
